Privacy Policy for KungKung Doctor (쿵쿵닥터)

Effective date: November 12, 2026
Last updated: November 12, 2026
Operator: SOUNDSUN (사운드선)
App: KungKung Doctor / 쿵쿵닥터 (Android, package com.soundsun.spl308)

KungKung Doctor is a reference app for measuring and logging everyday noise, such as noise between apartment floors, with your phone. SOUNDSUN ("we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. We do not operate a server, and the app does not send your measurements, records, recordings, photos, or videos to us.

1. Information We Handle

1.1 Data processed and stored only on your device

  • Microphone audio: Used to calculate sound levels (dB) in real time. Audio is saved as a WAV file in the app's private storage only when you tap Record (paid feature).
  • Camera (photos/videos): Photos or videos with the measured values are saved in the app's private storage only when you tap Photo or Video (paid feature).
  • Measurement records: Times and sound-level values of measurements you keep.
  • Noise diary: The sound type, place, situation, actions taken, and notes you type, plus the names of attached recordings, photos, or videos.
  • Calibration settings: Calibration values and preferences. The device manufacturer and model are read on the device only to choose a matching calibration preset.
  • Purchase status: Purchase information from Google Play is used to unlock the features you bought.

If Android backup is turned on, this data may be included in your Google account backup. That backup is managed by Google; we cannot access it.

1.2 Files you export

Measurement records (CSV) and recordings, photos, or videos leave the app only when you choose Export and pick a destination yourself (for example, a files app or cloud storage). We do not receive these files.

1.3 Data processed by Google AdMob / Play services (third party)

While you use the app for free, a banner ad is shown at the bottom of the screen. Once you make any purchase, ads are no longer shown. When ads are shown, Google may process:

  • Advertising ID, app set ID, and similar device identifiers
  • Device and app data used to serve, measure, and (where allowed) personalize ads
  • Approximate location that Google may infer from your IP address
  • Diagnostics / ad interaction data

Google uses this data to serve and measure ads, for analytics, and for fraud prevention and security. This is sharing with Google for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. We do not sell your information. See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps.

1.4 Google Play Billing

Paid features are one-time purchases through Google Play Billing. Payments and payment method details (such as card numbers) are handled directly by Google; the app does not collect or send payment information. Purchase and order records, such as the order number, purchased item, and amount, are Google Play's transaction records. SOUNDSUN can view order records in Google Play Console only as Google provides them, and uses them only for refunds and customer support.

1.5 Permissions

  • Microphone: Required for sound measurement and for recordings you choose to make.
  • Camera: Optional, only for photos and videos you choose to take.
  • Internet / network state: Ads over HTTPS and Google Play Billing.
  • Advertising ID: Google AdMob ads.
  • Billing: Google Play in-app purchases.

2. How We Use Information

  • On-device sound measurement, display, records, and diary
  • Storing calibration and UI preferences locally
  • Unlocking and verifying purchased features
  • Showing AdMob ads during free use (Google may process data as described above)

3. Sharing

  • Google (AdMob / Play services / Play Billing): The data above is processed by Google when ads are requested and purchases are made. Where required (for example, the EEA/UK), ad consent is collected via Google UMP.
  • Measurement audio, values, records, diary entries, recordings, photos, and videos are not sent to us.

4. Data Security

  • Measurement processing happens on the device
  • Records, diary entries, recordings, photos, and videos are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access
  • Ad, consent, and billing traffic uses HTTPS (cleartext HTTP is not permitted)

5. Retention and Deletion

  • In-app data is kept until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app
  • You can delete all measurement records, and delete diary entries with their attached files, in the app
  • Clearing the app's data in device settings or uninstalling the app removes everything
  • Files you export are managed and deleted by you
  • AdMob data retention follows Google's privacy policy

6. Your Rights

  • Revoke microphone or camera permission in system settings
  • Clear app data or uninstall the app to delete on-device data
  • EEA/UK: make your ad consent choice in the consent form (when shown)
  • Opt out of ad personalization and reset or delete your advertising ID in your Google account or device ad settings

This app has no user accounts. Because we do not hold your on-device data, we cannot view or delete it remotely; uninstalling the app removes it.

7. Recording and Filming Responsibly

Recording, photo, and video features are meant to document sounds heard in your own space and where they occur. Do not secretly record other people's conversations, film inside other people's homes, or otherwise use these features unlawfully. Measurements and records are for reference only and are not official measurements or legal determinations.

8. Age Requirement and Children

This app is intended for users aged 14 and over; it is not directed to children under 14. We do not knowingly process personal information of children under 14 (under Korea's Personal Information Protection Act, processing such information requires the consent of a legal guardian).

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
